`movie_B_crop.tif` is a 360 × 360 pixel crop (rows 221–580, columns 401–760) of movie B of
`data/kymographs_movies/`: microtubules growing from stabilized seeds with 6 µM tubulin and
100 mM KCl, without kinesin. Tubulin channel only, converted to 8 bits; 293 frames, one every 3 s;
0.11 µm per pixel. The calibration is stored in the file (ImageJ format).
